# Soil Acoustics

**Company, product and research review · 22 November 2026**

<blockquote class="governing-question">
<p>What is Soil Acoustics? What is the company? The background? The product? The science? The competition?</p>
</blockquote>

Soil Acoustics Ltd sells a way to listen for life in topsoil. A probe picks up vibrations from animals such as earthworms as they move or feed. This is one commercial application of **soil ecoacoustics**, the study of what belowground sounds can reveal about organisms and their environment. [SRC-001](sources.md#src-001), [SRC-002](sources.md#src-002), [SRC-023](sources.md#src-023)

## The company

Soil Acoustics Ltd is a private company based in Matlock, Derbyshire. It was incorporated on 16 February 2024. Companies House lists Andrew Baker, Saffron Baker and Gavin Ward as directors. Baker Consultants’ directors established the company to bring the meter to market. [SRC-005](sources.md#src-005), [SRC-006](sources.md#src-006), [SRC-007](sources.md#src-007)

## Research origins

Baker Consultants led a 2023–25 project with the University of Warwick. UK Research and Innovation records £257,758 in grant offers to the two partners. The team reports that it built a prototype and collected over 2,000 recordings. Sustainable Acoustics documented using the meter at Newhouse Farm in Hampshire. [SRC-008](sources.md#src-008), [SRC-009](sources.md#src-009), [SRC-010](sources.md#src-010)

## The product

The handheld **Soil Acoustic Meter (SAM)** records three minutes at each sampling point. The operator uploads the files, and the portal returns a proprietary **Soil Acoustic Quality Index (SAQI)** for each recording and a batch average. Regular UK packages cost **£1,010 for Light or £1,120 for Standard**, including one meter and the first year of analysis, before VAT and delivery. ## The science

In six South Australian woodland plots, researchers found that acoustic measures tracked differences in invertebrate communities. Soil moisture also affects how readily a sensor detects earthworm movement. The public studies reviewed give no independent estimate of how accurately commercial SAQI predicts animal counts on a new farm. A repeat recording paired with a worm count can help a farmer investigate a change. ## The competition

Biofonic is trialling a sensor that stays in the field and tracks conditions over time. Elekon sells the ALLSOUNDER ALISO for scheduled recordings and live listening. Frontier Labs sells a BAR-LT recorder with a compatible soil microphone. Both recorders leave the analysis to the user. The studies reviewed provide no head-to-head accuracy test across these systems.
