# Soil Acoustics

**Company, product and research review · 22 November 2026**

<blockquote class="governing-question">
<p>What can soil acoustic monitoring tell land managers about biological activity, and how do the available systems compare?</p>
</blockquote>

Soil Acoustics Ltd sells a handheld probe that listens to life in topsoil. Its strongest current use is to revisit a field and investigate changes in audible invertebrate activity. A quieter reading after a dry spell could mean that earthworms are less active, have moved deeper, or are harder to detect. A land manager needs soil conditions and an independent observation, such as a worm count, to interpret the change. [SRC-002](sources.md#src-002), [SRC-004](sources.md#src-004), [SRC-022](sources.md#src-022)

## Three findings

- **The company’s research origins are documented.** UKRI records £257,758 for a 2023–25 project led by Baker Consultants with the University of Warwick. Soil Acoustics Ltd was formed in 2024 to commercialise the technology. A named farm pilot and a reseller listing corroborate field use and distribution. [Company, funding and commercial offer](company-and-product.md) [SRC-008](sources.md#src-008)
- **The evidence is stronger for detecting activity than for assigning an overall health score.** Woodland and pasture studies found relationships between acoustic measures and invertebrates. The public evidence reviewed gives no independent estimate of the commercial Soil Acoustic Quality Index’s accuracy across farms, or of how big a change must be to exceed normal variation. [Research evidence and its limits](research-evidence.md) [SRC-019](sources.md#src-019)
- **The first website fix is to align the sampling instructions.** The website asks users to stand at least 1 m away and allow 4 minutes. The guide specifies a 3-minute recording after a 15-second delay, with the operator at least 2 m away. The two sources also give substantially different report turnaround times. [Website review and publisher pages](website-review.md) [SRC-002](sources.md#src-002), [SRC-003](sources.md#src-003)
