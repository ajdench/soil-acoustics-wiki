# Project status

## Current goal

Help a land manager interpret a soil recording, understand what the Soil Acoustic Meter supplies and compare it with alternative ways to collect or analyse field data.

## Current state

The [reader overview](../index.md) brings together the company record, the field guide, the independent science and the [systems comparison](../monitoring-systems.md). The [evidence matrix](../evidence-matrix.md) records which conclusions have support from field measurements and which rely on a supplier's description. Prices and retail availability have a review date of 22 November 2026.

## Active priorities

The current review and comparison cover the public evidence identified. The [validation queue](../validation-queue.md) records what would justify further work.
