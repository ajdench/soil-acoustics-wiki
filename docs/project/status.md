# Project status

## Current goal

Explain who developed the Soil Acoustic Meter, what its score can tell a land manager about soil life and how the service compares with other ways of monitoring a field.

## Current state

The [overview](../index.md) introduces the company and the question behind its product. The [company review](../company-and-product.md) follows the meter from funded research to commercial use. The [systems comparison](../monitoring-systems.md) records published prices and availability as checked on **22 November 2026**.

Two woodland studies found an association between acoustic measures and the number of animals collected. Their findings differed for the variety of animals. Both studies used research indices. The developer’s Denton Reserve report describes variation in acoustic complexity between sites. It gives no paired animal counts. A proposed Finnish forest study names the commercial meter and sets out a method for making that comparison. [Research evidence](../research-evidence.md) · [SRC-019](../sources.md#src-019), [SRC-045](../sources.md#src-045), [SRC-051](../sources.md#src-051), [SRC-054](../sources.md#src-054)

The [evidence matrix](../evidence-matrix.md) connects each conclusion to its sources. The [maintenance guide for editors and agents](../llm-wiki/index.md) explains how to update these records when new findings appear.
