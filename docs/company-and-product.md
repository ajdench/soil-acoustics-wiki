# The company and its product

Soil Acoustics Ltd commercialises a soil recorder and sells subscriptions to the analysis portal. Its origins are traceable through the UK research grant, company register and project reports.

## Origins and field use

| Question | What the public record supports |
| --- | --- |
| Who is behind it? | Soil Acoustics Ltd is an active private company in Matlock, Derbyshire, incorporated on 16 February 2024 (company 15493769). The register lists Andrew Baker, Saffron Baker and Gavin Ward as directors. Derbyshire Dales describes it as a company established by Baker Consultants’ directors to bring the meter to market. [SRC-005](sources.md#src-005), [SRC-006](sources.md#src-006), [SRC-007](sources.md#src-007) |
| Who funded development? | UKRI records £257,758 in public funding for April 2023 to March 2025. Baker Consultants led the project and received a grant offer of £116,565. The University of Warwick’s grant offer was £141,193. The commercial company was formed during the project. [SRC-008](sources.md#src-008) |
| What did the project produce? | The project team reports a prototype, more than 2,000 soil recordings, a machine-learning algorithm and controlled experiments. It names Rothamsted, Wildfarmed, the National Trust, JoJo’s Vineyard and Yeo Valley among the trial sites. These records establish development history. The number of recordings alone gives no estimate of score accuracy. [SRC-009](sources.md#src-009) |
| Is there evidence of use outside the developer? | Sustainable Acoustics describes deploying the meter in its Newhouse Farm work in Hampshire. That is first-hand evidence of field use. The account gives no paired soil counts or error estimates for the commercial score. The manufacturer also names Ruinart and Wildfarmed as clients. Its logo panel groups customers and partners together, so it cannot establish paying customer numbers or renewals. [SRC-010](sources.md#src-010), [SRC-002](sources.md#src-002) |

**Commercial position.** The published UK patent names Baker Consultants Ltd as the assignee and Andrew Baker and Carlos Abrahams as inventors. A reseller, Veldshop, lists the meter, and Natural Apptitude describes building the analysis portal. UK company filings include micro-entity accounts but do not disclose sales or paid subscriptions. [SRC-011](sources.md#src-011), [SRC-012](sources.md#src-012), [SRC-013](sources.md#src-013), [SRC-014](sources.md#src-014)

**Other approaches.** Biofonic is developing a sensor that can stay in a field for weeks and record moisture and temperature alongside sound. Its developer reports field trials and offers a waitlist. SAM’s commercial proposition is a portable meter that can visit multiple points in one session. These sampling designs answer different questions about spatial variation within a field and change through time at one point. [SRC-027](sources.md#src-027), [SRC-028](sources.md#src-028), [SRC-002](sources.md#src-002)

## What a buyer gets

The Soil Acoustic Meter (SAM) uses a contact microphone and a probe. The user records at several GPS-tagged points, uploads the files by computer, and receives a proprietary Soil Acoustic Quality Index (SAQI) for each recording and a mean for the batch. The portal can export comparisons as PDFs. The public guide shows the controls, location map and upload screens on pages 4–10. A worked scorecard with field observations would help readers understand the numbers. [SRC-003](sources.md#src-003)

| UK package | One meter and first year | Published annual subscription | Indicative monthly use |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Light | £1,010 | £180 | About 20 recordings, or two batches of ten |
| Standard | £1,120 | £290 | About 80 recordings, or eight batches of ten |
| Power | Quoted | Quoted | Agreed with the supplier |

The regular prices above exclude VAT and delivery. The supplier lists the meter at £830 plus VAT. The batch equivalents are calculated using the guide’s minimum of ten samples per setting. A ten-point visit entails 30 minutes of recording alone, before moving, setup and upload. A field with several distinct habitats may need multiple batches. The guide advises avoiding rain and nights below 10°C, and recommends wind below 10 mph. This can limit the number of comparable sampling days. [SRC-015](sources.md#src-015), [SRC-016](sources.md#src-016), [SRC-017](sources.md#src-017), [SRC-003](sources.md#src-003)

**Groundswell show offer.** On 22 November 2026 the shop also listed one meter and the first year of Standard access at **£830**, £290 below the regular package. The promotional page gave no end date. Eligibility and the final checkout price were unverified. [SRC-043](sources.md#src-043)

**Rights to data affect the choice of plan.** The published terms state that the company owns uploaded recordings, metadata and derived datasets. They grant users a limited licence for non-commercial and internal research or educational use of their copies and require written consent for use of SAQI or derived scores. Access to the portal depends on a valid subscription. A consultant promising a client a reusable dataset, or a researcher planning an open publication, should obtain written confirmation covering that use before collecting data. [SRC-018](sources.md#src-018)
