# Evidence matrix

The evidence state distinguishes public filings, supplier descriptions, field observations and research findings. An open evidence gap records the limits of the material reviewed on 22 November 2026.

| Claim | Support | Evidence state | Limit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Company identity and origins.** Soil Acoustics Ltd was incorporated in February 2024 to bring technology developed through Baker Consultants to market. | [SRC-005](sources.md#src-005), [SRC-006](sources.md#src-006), [SRC-007](sources.md#src-007) | Public record and council account | Changes to the board or registered status must be checked against the live register. |
| **Research funding.** UKRI lists £257,758 in grant offers to Baker Consultants and the University of Warwick for the soil ecoacoustics project. | [SRC-008](sources.md#src-008), [SRC-009](sources.md#src-009) | Public award record | This is research funding for the named recipients. It cannot be counted as the spin-out’s revenue. |
| **Development trials.** The project team reports controlled experiments, a prototype and over 2,000 soil recordings. | [SRC-009](sources.md#src-009) | Reported by the project team | A count of recordings says little about the number of independent sites or how accurately SAQI predicts fauna on unseen farms. |
| **Evidence of use and distribution.** An outside monitoring team describes deploying a SAM at Newhouse Farm. A specialist retailer lists the product and the portal developer describes its implementation. | [SRC-010](sources.md#src-010), [SRC-012](sources.md#src-012), [SRC-013](sources.md#src-013) | Corroborated use and distribution | Buyer numbers, renewals and commercial turnover cannot be inferred from listings, testimonials or logo panels. |
| **UK patent.** The granted UK patent names Baker Consultants Ltd as assignee and Andrew Baker and Carlos Abrahams as inventors. | [SRC-011](sources.md#src-011) | Published patent | Patent scope, later assignments and the commercial company's licence are distinct questions. The grant says nothing about predictive accuracy. |
| **Sampling and interpretation.** The April 2026 guide describes three-minute recordings, a W-shaped route and at least ten samples per setting. The device records location and the portal calculates scores by recording and batch. | [SRC-002](sources.md#src-002), [SRC-003](sources.md#src-003), [SRC-004](sources.md#src-004) | Manufacturer instructions | An average conceals differences between sampling points. Rain, temperature, wind and operator position affect comparability. |
| **Cost of a field visit.** The supplier's listed regular UK packages are £1,010 for Light and £1,120 for Standard with one meter and first-year access, excluding tax and shipping. | [SRC-003](sources.md#src-003), [SRC-015](sources.md#src-015), [SRC-016](sources.md#src-016), [SRC-017](sources.md#src-017), [SRC-043](sources.md#src-043) | Published price and calculation | At ten points, recording alone takes 30 minutes. Setup, travel and difficult plots add time. The Groundswell show listing displayed £830 for the first Standard year with hardware. It gave no end date. Eligibility and checkout were unverified. |
| **Data ownership and reuse.** The public terms reserve uploaded data to the company, limit users' licence for copies and require written consent for score use. Access depends on a current subscription. | [SRC-018](sources.md#src-018) | Published supplier terms | A separate written agreement could grant additional rights. Public sales copy does not settle a particular client's permission to share. |
| **A signal in woodland restoration.** Acoustic measures differed between cleared and revegetated or remnant woodland and were associated with manual invertebrate observations in one South Australian study. | [SRC-019](sources.md#src-019), [SRC-034](sources.md#src-034) | Peer-reviewed field evidence | Six plots in one landscape over five days. The measures were research indices and were not the commercial SAQI. |
| **Event counts and sound diversity.** In test recordings with known properties, combinations of indices tracked sound-event counts better than sound evenness or pooled diversity. | [SRC-020](sources.md#src-020) | Peer-reviewed experimental evidence | More sound events need not mean more animals or species. The constructed recordings cannot validate claims in all field conditions. |
| **Associations in UK pasture.** Several acoustic indices correlated with the number of earthworms collected at ten Oxfordshire grassland sites. | [SRC-021](sources.md#src-021) | Preliminary field evidence | A 24-hour research recording differs from a three-minute SAM sample. Management, soils and moisture can confound comparisons. |
| **Soil moisture and detection.** Earthworm-induced vibrations were detected more frequently in wetter soil in a controlled experiment. | [SRC-022](sources.md#src-022) | Peer-reviewed experimental evidence | One apparatus and selected earthworm groups. Results do not separate every biological or physical cause of a field change. |
| **Independent calibration of SAQI.** The public material reviewed does not provide independent estimates of commercial SAQI accuracy on unseen farms or its minimum detectable change. | [SRC-002](sources.md#src-002), [SRC-009](sources.md#src-009), [SRC-010](sources.md#src-010), [SRC-019](sources.md#src-019), [SRC-020](sources.md#src-020), [SRC-021](sources.md#src-021), [SRC-023](sources.md#src-023) | Open evidence gap | This finding is bounded by the sources and review date. Unpublished studies or a later validation could change it. |
| **Scope of a soil health assessment.** The AHDB framework combines structure, pH, nutrients, earthworms and organic matter. Repeated recordings provide an additional biological observation. | [SRC-003](sources.md#src-003), [SRC-021](sources.md#src-021), [SRC-024](sources.md#src-024) | Published framework and interpretation | An acoustic statistic alone does not measure each chemical, physical and biological indicator in the scorecard. |
| **Field protocol and report timing.** Public pages and guide v6 differ on distance from the probe, how long to wait and when to expect results. | [SRC-001](sources.md#src-001), [SRC-002](sources.md#src-002), [SRC-003](sources.md#src-003), [SRC-004](sources.md#src-004) | Documented discrepancy | Four minutes of quiet could include preparation as well as recording. The sources do not give a common breakdown or observed processing time. |
| **Purchase and portal journey.** Different Buy Now links lead to a collection or plan comparison. The public portal shows sign-in and a purchase route. | [SRC-001](sources.md#src-001), [SRC-015](sources.md#src-015), [SRC-025](sources.md#src-025), [SRC-026](sources.md#src-026) | Observed public interface | Inspections covered the public web flow. They supply no evidence of actual checkout completion, account results or physical handset usability. |
| **A commercially available soil recorder.** Elekon introduced the ALLSOUNDER ALISO in March 2023. It supports scheduled recordings, live listening and local file export. Swiss Sounding Soil identifies it as its loan device. | [SRC-029](sources.md#src-029), [SRC-030](sources.md#src-030), [SRC-031](sources.md#src-031), [SRC-032](sources.md#src-032), [SRC-040](sources.md#src-040) | Manufacturer, retailer and programme operator | Hardware pricing and local loan conditions differ from a subscription that delivers benchmarked reports. |
| **A research assembly and a current microphone.** Robinson and colleagues describe a JrF C-Series Pro contact microphone, metal probe and Zoom H4n Pro. JrF also supplies an Ecoutic microphone with interchangeable probes. | [SRC-033](sources.md#src-033), [SRC-034](sources.md#src-034) | Published methods and manufacturer listing | The quoted study system cost is historical. Today's Ecoutic price covers a different component and excludes a recorder and analysis. |
| **A low-cost programmable logger.** A tundra experiment recorded with AudioMoth devices in tubes at 10 cm depth. The current v1.2.0 board is sold separately from cases and field accessories. | [SRC-035](sources.md#src-035), [SRC-036](sources.md#src-036) | Published methods and supplier listing | Sound transmission changed with soil and vegetation. The recorded soundscapes were more informative about treatments than daily worm activity. |
| **A trial-stage monitoring system.** Biofonic describes long-duration field sensors for sound and environmental measurements. The public record includes an R&D award, a farm trial account and a waitlist. | [SRC-027](sources.md#src-027), [SRC-028](sources.md#src-028), [SRC-038](sources.md#src-038), [SRC-039](sources.md#src-039) | Developer-reported capability and field participation | Reported deployment, the grant and recruitment do not establish retail availability, price or validated biodiversity scores. |
| **A research array for changing soil conditions.** Maeder and colleagues recorded Swiss forest soils using custom contact microphones and periodic automated recording, alongside faunal and environmental sampling. | [SRC-037](sources.md#src-037) | Peer-reviewed field evidence | The dedicated research array cannot be assumed to have the characteristics or validation of a retail device bearing a related programme name. |
| **A field recorder with a dedicated soil microphone.** Frontier Labs lists the BAR-LT at A$930 and its soil microphone at A$350. The advertised components total A$1,280 before taxes and field accessories. | [SRC-041](sources.md#src-041), [SRC-042](sources.md#src-042) | Manufacturer specifications and price calculation | Compatibility and recording features are documented. Validation of the exact pair against independent fauna counts was not identified in the sources reviewed. |
