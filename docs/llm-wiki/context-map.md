# Context map

Choose the row for the question you need to answer. The reader pages explain the topic. For a factual claim, follow the citations back to the publication and check its scope.

## Answer a subject question

| Question | Start here | What to check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| What does the meter do, and who developed it? | [Overview](../index.md) and [company and product](../company-and-product.md) | Separate the university research project, Baker Consultants and Soil Acoustics Ltd. Attribute commercial claims to their publisher. |
| What can a land manager buy or borrow? | [Monitoring systems](../monitoring-systems.md) and [source register](../sources.md) | Check the date, territory, currency, tax, included equipment, analysis costs and support. Distinguish a price from an invitation to join a trial. |
| What does a field result establish? | [What soil sounds tell us](../research-evidence.md) and [evidence matrix](../evidence-matrix.md) | Identify the habitat, recorder, index, organisms and independent field observations. Check whether the work tested the commercial score. |
| Which field instructions were published? | [Website and field guide](../website-review.md) | Check the guide version and observation date. Use the [validation queue](../validation-queue.md) for unresolved differences. |
| Where can I trace a claim? | [Evidence matrix](../evidence-matrix.md), [source register](../sources.md) and the relevant concept under `knowledge/` | The [graph](../graph/index.md) can find related records. Check the records themselves before drawing a conclusion. |

## Maintain the wiki

| Task | Start here | What to check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Write original English prose | The manifest's `roles.authoring_policy` path, or `AUTHORING.md` when no path is mapped | Apply the DenchCo ASD-STE100-inspired 80% house style. Preserve protected text and source meaning, follow stricter local policy and record material exceptions. |
| Change a source or reader page | [Maintenance guide](maintenance.md), manifest, `AGENTS.md` and [research log](../log.md) | Update the source and evidence records before rebuilding the pages. Consult the design and runtime contracts when the change affects them. |
| Link pages in a development response | [Development response navigation](maintenance.md#development-response-navigation) and the canonical Human Wiki URL in the manifest | Check Markdown, HTML and plain GFM links in the captured draft. Managed local links require `--managed-live` and a verified service and route. |
| Select follow-up work | [Project status](../project/status.md), [validation queue](../validation-queue.md) and handoff evidence | Address required checks first. Reopen parked items when their stated condition is met. |
| Propose a reusable change | [Standard proposal workflow](standard-proposals.md), verified implementation commit and immutable Standard pin | Retain the evidence, regression fixture, migration impact and approval for the exact payload. Check the separate authorities for submission, release and adoption. |
