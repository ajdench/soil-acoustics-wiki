# Website and field guide review

A visitor can hear underground activity on the homepage and follow the field guide’s diagrams from probe placement to upload. The harder question is what to do with the next reading. The website and guide give different instructions for taking a sample, and neither shows how a farmer might interpret a change in score. [SRC-001](sources.md#src-001), [SRC-002](sources.md#src-002), [SRC-003](sources.md#src-003)

## Changes to make first

1. **Give operators one field procedure.** *Learn More* says to stand at least 1 m away and stay quiet for 4 minutes. The April 2026 guide specifies at least 2 m and a three-minute recording after a 15-second delay. The September field advice also recommends about 2 m. Noise from an operator’s movement can affect a reading. Publish the recommended distance, delay and recording time together wherever an operator starts a sample, including the quick-start card. [SRC-002](sources.md#src-002), [SRC-003](sources.md#src-003), [SRC-004](sources.md#src-004)

2. **Show what a change in score might mean.** Soil Acoustics says a higher Soil Acoustic Quality Index (SAQI) corresponds to more invertebrates and a greater variety. The guide shows how to compare averages. An anonymised example could show the ten readings behind each average, the dates, soil moisture and earthworm counts at the same points. Explain what the observations support. If repeat visits have yet to establish how much scores normally vary, say so. Link to independent validation where available. [SRC-002](sources.md#src-002), [SRC-003](sources.md#src-003)

3. **Say when a result will be ready.** The homepage says automated reports will be available after 48 hours. The guide says a report is available almost immediately after upload. If a recording score and a downloadable report arrive at different times, give both timings. Tell the operator when a delay warrants contacting support. [SRC-001](sources.md#src-001), [SRC-003](sources.md#src-003)

4. **Put the price beside the permission to use the results.** A consultant choosing Standard can see that the plan covers several sites. To find out whether a client may reuse a score, the consultant must also read the terms. For each plan, show the first-year price, renewal, number of ten-point visits and uses that require written consent. The [company and product page](company-and-product.md#what-a-buyer-gets) brings these details together. [SRC-017](sources.md#src-017), [SRC-018](sources.md#src-018)

## The buyer’s route through the website

1. **Discover the meter.** The homepage offers a recording visitors can play before reading about the instrument. In Safari, some carousel images exposed filenames such as `AdobeStock_137392838` to assistive technology. Give informative logos the organisation’s name and use empty alternative text for decorative photos. [SRC-001](sources.md#src-001)

    [Visit the Soil Acoustic Meter homepage](https://soilacoustics.com/)

2. **Open Buy Now in the header.** The link opens a shop collection headed *Home page*. It presents Light and Standard meter bundles beside a spare probe. A heading such as “Meters and spare parts” and a link to the plan comparison would help a first-time buyer choose. [SRC-025](sources.md#src-025)

    [View the meter and probe collection](https://shop.soilacoustics.com/collections/frontpage)

3. **Open Buy Now in the footer.** The footer link opens a three-plan comparison with annual pricing. Buyers looking for that comparison from the header take a different route. Use a shared destination or distinct labels such as “Compare plans” and “Shop meters and spares”. [SRC-015](sources.md#src-015)

    [Read the supplier’s plan comparison](https://soilacoustics.com/buy-now/)

4. **Look for a sample report.** The public portal opens at an email and password sign-in. Its link for visitors without an account leads to a purchase. A sample report beside the sign-in would show a farmer what the subscription delivers. [SRC-026](sources.md#src-026)

    [View the customer portal sign-in form](https://portal.soilacoustics.com/login)

5. **Navigate at a narrow width.** In a narrow Safari window, the menu opened and displayed Learn More, News, Login and Buy Now. This check covered the layout. Touch targets and screen-reader announcements would need tests on a handset with assistive technology. [SRC-001](sources.md#src-001)

    [Open the homepage to inspect its responsive menu](https://soilacoustics.com/)

This review covers the public pages and version 6 of the guide, checked on 22 November 2026. The descriptions of automated analysis, timing and paid features come from the supplier. The review stopped at the public sign-in and shop listings. For evidence on interpreting a field score, see [What soil sounds tell us](research-evidence.md).
