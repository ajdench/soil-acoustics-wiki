# Website and field guide review

The sound examples give visitors a way to hear the phenomenon for themselves. The guide’s device diagram, sampling pattern and portal screenshots make the process concrete. The main difficulty comes when a visitor asks how to take a comparable reading, what a score means or what the total service costs. [SRC-001](sources.md#src-001), [SRC-002](sources.md#src-002), [SRC-003](sources.md#src-003)

## Changes to make first

| Priority | Observed issue | Change |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Align field instructions | Learn More asks the operator to stand at least 1 m away and stay quiet for 4 minutes. The April guide specifies at least 2 m and a 3-minute recording after a 15-second delay. The September field advice also says about 2 m. An operator following the web instructions could introduce footstep or clothing noise by standing closer. [SRC-002](sources.md#src-002), [SRC-003](sources.md#src-003), [SRC-004](sources.md#src-004) | Publish one versioned field protocol. Distinguish recording length from the total time on site, and reproduce the same figures on the web and in the quick-start card. |
| 2. Explain the score | The site links a higher score to greater invertebrate abundance and variety. The guide shows users how to compare batch averages, but gives no worked example of how much change in the Soil Acoustic Quality Index (SAQI) merits investigation. [SRC-002](sources.md#src-002), [SRC-003](sources.md#src-003) | Publish one anonymised field comparison with the ten individual scores, dates, moisture, earthworm counts and a plain-language interpretation. Identify where independent validation is available. |
| 3. Set one turnaround expectation | The homepage promises reports after 48 hours. The guide’s introduction describes reports arriving almost immediately. A buyer cannot tell when to expect a score or whether a delayed upload needs support. [SRC-001](sources.md#src-001), [SRC-003](sources.md#src-003) | Give the typical time for a batch score and any separate time for a report. State when a user should contact support. |
| 4. Put cost and permissions together | The pages for agronomists and researchers describe shareable reports and multiple sites. The costs and rights governing reuse of scores sit on separate pages. [SRC-017](sources.md#src-017), [SRC-018](sources.md#src-018) | Show first-year and renewal costs, typical ten-point visits per plan, and which client or publication uses are already permitted. |

## The buyer’s route through the website

1. **Discover the meter.** The homepage names the instrument and offers a recording of underground activity. In Safari’s accessibility tree, some carousel images had asset-style names such as `AdobeStock_137392838`. Decorative photos can use empty alternative text. Partner logos that convey information should use the organisation’s name. [SRC-001](sources.md#src-001)

    [Visit the Soil Acoustic Meter homepage](https://soilacoustics.com/)

2. **Open Buy Now in the header.** It leads to a product collection headed *Home page*. Visitors see the Light and Standard bundles beside a spare probe before getting an explanation of how to choose a plan. A descriptive heading and a link to the comparison would make this route easier to use. [SRC-025](sources.md#src-025)

    [View the meter and probe collection](https://shop.soilacoustics.com/collections/frontpage)

3. **Open Buy Now in the footer.** The same label leads to a different page with a more informative three-plan comparison, including renewal pricing. Give both purchase links a common destination, or label the two destinations “Compare plans” and “Shop meters and spares”. [SRC-015](sources.md#src-015)

    [Read the supplier’s plan comparison](https://soilacoustics.com/buy-now/)

4. **Preview a result.** The portal opens at an email and password sign-in. The link for a visitor without an account leads to a purchase. A sample report here would let a farmer evaluate the output before buying. [SRC-026](sources.md#src-026)

    [View the customer portal sign-in form](https://portal.soilacoustics.com/login)

5. **Navigate at a narrow width.** Opening the responsive menu exposed the Learn More, News, Login and Buy Now links. This worked in a narrow-layout Safari check. A physical handset and screen reader would be needed to test touch targets and announcements directly. [SRC-001](sources.md#src-001)

    [Open the homepage to inspect its responsive menu](https://soilacoustics.com/)

The observations cover public pages and manufacturer documentation. Automated analysis, report turnaround and paid account features are described from the guide. No customer login or purchase was used. For evidence on what the score can tell a farmer, see [What soil sounds tell us](research-evidence.md).
