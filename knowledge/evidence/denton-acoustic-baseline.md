# What the Denton baseline can show

Baker Consultants reports a statistically significant difference in acoustic complexity among ten sites within Denton Reserve during a July 2025 baseline survey.

**Evidence.** Developer-reported field comparison.

**Limit.** The authors describe pre-existing differences between habitats. They give no per-site scores, paired animal counts or error estimates and do not name the index used for this result.
