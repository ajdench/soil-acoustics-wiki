# Abundance and richness in English woodland

In six South Yorkshire plots, both the Acoustic Complexity Index and the Bioacoustic Index were associated with the abundance of hand-sorted invertebrates. Neither showed a statistically significant association with taxon richness.

**Evidence.** Peer-reviewed field and chamber evidence.

**Limit.** The two indices distinguished restoration treatments in chamber recordings. The in-ground comparison did not detect those differences. Hand sorting counted visible groups within one woodland.
