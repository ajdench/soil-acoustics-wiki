# Checking which animal made a sound

Researchers excavated 28 symptomatic grapevine root systems after recording them. Twenty-seven contained invertebrates, but the team recovered only one live grape root borer larva.

**Evidence.** Field observations checked by excavation.

**Limit.** The investigators classified a vine as infested when it contained a sound-producing invertebrate. This comparison could not establish how often the detector correctly identified the target pest.
