# Cost of a field visit

The supplier's listed regular UK packages are £1,010 for Light and £1,120 for Standard with one meter and first-year access, excluding tax and shipping.

**Evidence.** Published price and calculation.

**Limit.** At ten points, recording alone takes 30 minutes. Setup, travel and difficult plots add time. The Groundswell show listing displayed £830 for the first Standard year with hardware. It gave no end date. Eligibility and checkout were unverified.
