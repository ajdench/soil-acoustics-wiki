# Event counts and sound diversity

In test recordings with known properties, combinations of indices tracked sound-event counts better than sound evenness or pooled diversity.

**Evidence.** Peer-reviewed experimental evidence.

**Limit.** More sound events need not mean more animals or species. The constructed recordings cannot validate claims in all field conditions.
