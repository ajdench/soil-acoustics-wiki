# Earthworm pits as a field reference

In a check of 27 soil pits on nine fields, a second five-minute hand sort found an average of 1.6 extra earthworms per pit. Juveniles made up 91% of these additional finds.

**Evidence.** Peer-reviewed farmer survey and repeat sorting.

**Limit.** The survey counted earthworms and grouped the adults by their ecology. It did not test an acoustic recorder or validate the commercial Soil Acoustic Quality Index.
