# Separating movement sounds under controlled conditions

Six species differed overall in their acoustic profiles on an aluminium plate, with no significant pairwise separation between garden worms and snails.

**Evidence.** Peer-reviewed experimental evidence.

**Limit.** The animals were recorded separately on a uniform surface. The analysis does not give species-identification accuracy for mixed field recordings.
