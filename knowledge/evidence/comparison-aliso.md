# A commercially available soil recorder

Elekon introduced the ALLSOUNDER ALISO in March 2023. It supports scheduled recordings, live listening and local file export. Swiss Sounding Soil identifies it as its loan device.

**Evidence.** Manufacturer, retailer and programme operator.

**Limit.** Hardware pricing and local loan conditions differ from a subscription that delivers benchmarked reports.
