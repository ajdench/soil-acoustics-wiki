# Scope of a soil health assessment

The AHDB framework combines structure, pH, nutrients, earthworms and organic matter. Repeated recordings provide an additional biological observation.

**Evidence.** Published framework and interpretation.

**Limit.** An acoustic statistic alone does not measure each chemical, physical and biological indicator in the scorecard.
