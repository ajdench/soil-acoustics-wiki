Baker Consultants' June 2026 patent application reports 89% accuracy for an example model that recognises a wireworm sound in short recordings.

**Evidence.** Applicant-reported classifier result.

**Limit.** The application does not say that separate farms were held back for testing. It gives no accuracy estimate for the commercial SAQI score.
