# A low-cost programmable logger

A tundra experiment recorded with AudioMoth devices in tubes at 10 cm depth. The current v1.2.0 board is sold separately from cases and field accessories.

**Evidence.** Published methods and supplier listing.

**Limit.** Sound transmission changed with soil and vegetation. The recorded soundscapes were more informative about treatments than daily worm activity.
