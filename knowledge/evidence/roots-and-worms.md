# Sources of underground vibrations

Earthworm burrowing and maize root growth each produced acoustic emissions in controlled soil experiments.

**Evidence.** Peer-reviewed experimental evidence.

**Limit.** Specialist ultrasonic sensors and small experimental cells. The experiment does not establish what fraction of a SAM recording comes from roots or worms.
