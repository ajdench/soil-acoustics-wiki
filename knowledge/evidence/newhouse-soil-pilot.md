# What the Newhouse Farm pilot reported

Sustainable Acoustics reports deploying the soil meter at Newhouse Farm. The farm open-day comparison concerned birds and bats, and the Institute of Acoustics scheduled a talk on the air and soil pilot for 14 September 2026.

**Evidence.** Operator account and conference programme.

**Limit.** The public account and programme give no paired soil-fauna counts or SAQI error estimates. The review located no public conference paper reporting soil results from this pilot.
