# One species can raise an acoustic index

Increasing the number of red wood ants raised four acoustic measures in controlled recordings with no change in species richness.

**Evidence.** Peer-reviewed experimental evidence.

**Limit.** The group sizes and detection limits depend on the ant species, substrate, equipment and experimental background. They do not calibrate a mixed field community or SAQI.
