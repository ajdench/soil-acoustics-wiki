# The original sampling problem

Warwick and Baker set out to shorten earthworm surveys by recording soil animals in place and comparing the sounds with a reference library.

**Evidence.** Project partners' stated aim.

**Limit.** The 2023 announcement describes a feasibility study and pilot recordings. It gives no conversion between a recording and a worm count.
