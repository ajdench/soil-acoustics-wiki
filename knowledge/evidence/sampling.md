# Sampling and interpretation

The April 2026 guide describes three-minute recordings, a W-shaped route and at least ten samples per setting. The device records location and the portal calculates scores by recording and batch.

**Evidence.** Manufacturer instructions.

**Limit.** An average conceals differences between sampling points. Rain, temperature, wind and operator position affect comparability.
