# A signal in woodland restoration

Acoustic measures differed between cleared and revegetated or remnant woodland and were associated with manual invertebrate observations in one South Australian study.

**Evidence.** Peer-reviewed field evidence.

**Limit.** Six plots in one landscape over five days. The measures were research indices and were not the commercial SAQI.
