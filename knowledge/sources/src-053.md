# Acoustics 2026 final conference programme

Page 3 lists a Hampshire case study of acoustics above and below ground by K. Baker, A. Officer and P. Rogers of Sustainable Acoustics. The Environmental Sound session in Peterborough scheduled it for 16:00 on Monday 14 September 2026. The monitoring team's earlier account names Newhouse Farm and the three authors.

**Interpretation.** The programme establishes the scheduled slot and subject. It contains no soil measurements. A search of the Institute's public catalogue by title, farm and author, and of the monitoring team's public news, located no full paper reporting results for the soil meter as of 22 November 2026.
