# The efficacy of acoustic indices for monitoring abundance and diversity in soil soundscapes, 2024

Fourteen indices tested against known event counts and sound types in synthetic one-minute soundscapes.

**Interpretation.** Constructed recordings isolate acoustic diversity, which differs from taxonomic diversity. A coauthor was affiliated with Baker Consultants.
