# The sound of restored soil in a temperate forest, 2023

Researchers compared acoustic indices and manual invertebrate observations in restored and deforested plots at Greno Woods, South Yorkshire.

**Interpretation.** See the results on animal abundance, richness, chamber recordings and in-ground recordings. One coauthor listed Baker Consultants as an affiliation. The study used a JrF contact microphone and a Zoom recorder.
