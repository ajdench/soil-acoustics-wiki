# Soil Ecoacoustics — Listening In to Soil Health

Baker Consultants describes a commissioned baseline survey at Denton Reserve in West Yorkshire and a separate pilot comparing farming systems in 11 countries.

The complete article appeared in the Chartered Institute of Ecology and Environmental Management’s *In Practice*, issue 132, June 2026, pages 33–37. The authors provide a public copy through the link at the end of their announcement.

**Interpretation.** Box 2 on page 36 describes the commercial meter and SAQI. The Denton case on pages 36–37 describes a commissioned survey across ten sites on two visits and reports a difference in acoustic complexity, without specifying the index, recorder model or site-level values. The authors attribute this baseline variation to existing site differences. Separately, page 36 reports about 6,000 records in a 2025 pilot across 11 countries. It gives no farm count, direction or magnitude of the difference between farming systems, or independent biological measurements for that comparison.
