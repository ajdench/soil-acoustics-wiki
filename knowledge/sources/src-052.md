# Grape root borer acoustic detection, final report for GS09-082

Researchers recorded around 28 vines showing symptoms and excavated their roots within two hours to check what was present.

**Interpretation.** Twenty-seven root systems contained invertebrates, and only one live grape root borer larva was recovered. For its acoustic assessment the team grouped sound-producing invertebrates together. The trial concerned these selected vines and this research equipment.
