# Soil health pilot study in England: Outcomes from an on-farm earthworm survey

Jacqueline L. Stroud, *PLOS ONE*, 2019. DOI 10.1371/journal.pone.0203909. Farmers surveyed 126 fields covering 1,318 hectares in England in spring 2018. They hand-sorted soil from ten pits per field and recorded total earthworm numbers and adult ecological groups.

**Interpretation.** The paper's quality check used 27 pits from nine fields. A second sort recovered juveniles that observers had missed on the first pass. It measured the limits of the manual count. There was no paired acoustic measurement.
