# Acoustic differentiation of soil-surface-dwelling invertebrates under controlled conditions, 2026

Six invertebrate species were recorded individually on an aluminium plate, using 19 acoustic features to compare movements.

**Interpretation.** The researchers recorded ten individuals per species for five minutes each. Their multivariate comparison describes differences among known groups. They did not test identification of unknown individuals in field soil.
