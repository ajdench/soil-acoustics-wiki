# Soil monitoring, UK patent application GB2702686A

The application published on 24 June 2026 describes recognising the rhythmic sound of wireworms in soil recordings.

**Interpretation.** Paragraphs 83 and 84 report training and testing on labelled sound clips and give an accuracy of 89%. The document does not identify a test on separate farms or an error rate for the commercial SAQI score.
