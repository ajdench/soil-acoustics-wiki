# Continuous measurement of red wood ant outdoor behaviour using passive acoustic monitoring, 2024

Calibrated red wood ant group sizes were recorded in the laboratory, followed by measurements at active and abandoned ant mounds in Sweden.

**Interpretation.** See the experimental design and Figure 1. Thirty laboratory recordings across six densities isolate the effect of changing the number of individuals of one species.
