# Non-native species change the tune of tundra soils, 2022

Thirty-six AudioMoth loggers were placed in tubes beneath tundra soils to compare effects of earthworm presence.

**Interpretation.** Differences depended on vegetation and sound transmission. Daily earthworm movement was difficult to infer.
