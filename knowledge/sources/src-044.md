# Listening to earthworms burrowing and roots growing, 2018

Time-lapse observations and ultrasonic sensors tracked acoustic events from earthworm burrowing and maize root growth in experimental soil.

**Interpretation.** The glass-cell experiment compared one worm with background and soil controls. The physical mechanism is relevant to signal interpretation. The apparatus had a 40–450 kHz sensor range and did not measure commercial SAQI.
