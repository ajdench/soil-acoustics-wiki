# TREES doctoral project on forest soil biodiversity and the Soil Acoustic Meter

A proposed Royal Holloway-led project would compare SAM recordings with conventional soil fauna sampling in the Satakunta forest experiment in Finland.

**Interpretation.** Soil Acoustics Ltd is the named industry partner. The project describes sampling single-species and mixed tree plots and comparing acoustic indices. It gives objectives and no completed comparisons.
